Each board is tested against the values listed in the columns ``Min'' and
``Max''. ``Typical'' is the mean value of the first 10 boards produced or a
value that is set by design.

\section{Dimensions}

The xHPTDC8 can be installed in most full-height PCIe slots and occupies one slot widths.

The dimensions, excluding the slot bracket, are
\textbf{167.6~$\times$~120.4~$\times$~21.6~mm} (depths $\times$ height $\times$ width).

Only the external-clock coax connector protrudes above the maximum allowed PCIe
height of 111.15~mm (see J2 in Figure~\ref{fig:schematics}).
The center of the connector is placed 44.4~mm from the front edge
of the PCB and protrudes 9.25~mm beyond PCIe specifications.
